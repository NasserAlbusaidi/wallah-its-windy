What this model knows

Observed inputs

OISST sea-surface temperatures, ERA5 steering winds and shear, IBTrACS tracks, and GMRT terrain shape the sandbox.

Deliberate simplifications

Track and intensity remain a point-storm model. A parametric Holland wind field derives pressure, eyewall scale, and quadrant wind radii; those are simulated structure, not aircraft observations. Dry air is a geometric coastal proxy. Wadi light follows timed HydroSHEDS D8 routes, but it is not a discharge or inundation model.

Physical structure

The amber ring marks radius of maximum wind. Cyan contours show the parametric 34- and 64-knot footprints. Forward motion strengthens the right side. The RMW climatology is an Atlantic-derived proxy, explicitly bounded for this North Indian Ocean sandbox.

Historic environments

Gonu and Shaheen modes replay their environmental timelines around your simulated vortex. The faint line is the observed track. These are counterfactual experiments, not historical reconstructions.
